A new look for the Mac app: the chrome now sits on a night sky, with panels floating over a ground and a horizon along the window's foot. Pick between two darks, Moonlit (the familiar charcoal, with a pixel moon) and Midnight (pure black for OLED screens, stars only). Each workspace gets its own planet, star or moon, which animates while an agent works.

## What's new

### Desktop

- **Moonlit and Midnight.** Settings → Appearance now offers a choice of dark: Moonlit, today's softer charcoal and the default, or Midnight, pure black as on the iPhone app. System follows macOS, using your dark theme at night. Light keeps the dawn hills.
- **Panels on a ground.** The sidebar, conversation and changes sit as rounded panels on the window's ground, under a pill title bar, with the theme's sky along the bottom.
- **Celestials.** Every workspace is assigned a pixel planet, star or moon that stays with it. Its agent sessions carry it, and it animates while a session is working.
- **The conversation.** The session header shows its celestial, title, harness, state, turn and branch. Finished turns show their summary, and hovering one shows what it did ("2 files · 2 commands · 1m 41s") with copy and fork. The composer has a notice strip for approvals, queued follow-ups and a nearly full context, a working paperclip for attaching files, running subagents as moons, a context ring and the permission mode.
- **Instrument cards.** The model picker, the session context card, constellation stats, the Hosts card (from the machine bar) and the new Needs You inbox (from the title-bar badge) are redesigned as instrument cards. Menus, the jump menu and toasts are plainer, and popovers always fit inside the window.
- **Approvals show the whole request.** The Needs You inbox and the composer's strip never cut a command short: you approve from the full request.
- **Review.** The queue and the open pull request's files sit on the left, the diff in the centre and the Risk Summary card on the right. Asking the reviewer swaps the findings for the conversation. Submitting with a Critical finding open asks once first. The stack popover draws the stack as layers tinted by state (merged, open, draft, closed).
- **Constellations.** A constellation's glance is a star chart, with its tasks grouped in bands, and a folded lead row shows a wave bar of its progress.
- **The editor.** The status bar sits at the foot of the editor card, and moves under the terminal when it opens. Vim modes are coloured badges, the branch truncates in the middle, and a floating chip opens the terminal. The tab completion card is redesigned, and its model list no longer repeats a model.
- **Settings.** Appearance previews each theme with its sky; Usage's plan limits and tokens are instrument cards.
- **Claude Code titles.** Claude sessions take the title Claude Code generates for them.
- **Sturdier on long and odd content.** Long titles, branches and host names, right-to-left text, huge counts and narrow windows were tested across every view; they truncate with tooltips instead of clipping.

### Your hosts

- **No burst of stale pushes when you leave.** While you're at the Mac, a host now holds only Needs You for your phone. Finished turns and notifications, which the Mac already showed, are no longer sent when you step away.

These are in build 7.

- A thread shows every turn in full instead of collapsing earlier ones, and loads older turns as you scroll up.

## Installing

**Mac (Apple silicon).** Download `Polaris-0.5.0-arm64.dmg` and drag Polaris to Applications, then open it.

- **On 0.4.3 or earlier,** Polaris updates itself. Choose **Restart to update** when it says 0.5.0 is ready.

**Windows.** The Windows build still isn't signed, so SmartScreen will warn you the first time. Download `Polaris-0.5.0-x64-win-Setup.exe` (or the `arm64` one for ARM PCs) and run it. When SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

- **On 0.2.0 or later,** Polaris updates itself.

**Your hosts.** With **Keep daemons up to date** on (Settings → Hosts), each host moves to 0.5.0 the next time it connects. Workspace celestials and Claude Code's titles need the host on 0.5.0.

## Known limitations

- With many editor tabs open, nothing shows that more lie past the edge of the tab strip.
- Undo, a live edit's small diff and the goal notice in the composer wait on later work.
- The iPhone app is in TestFlight, by invitation.
- 0.3.0's [known limitations](https://github.com/luxdotdev/polaris/releases/tag/v0.3.0) still apply.
