Quieter phone, faster typing: while you're at a Mac running Polaris, your hosts hold pushes to your phone and send them only if you step away without handling them. On the Mac, ⌘N puts you straight in the new session's prompt, and typing anywhere on a session screen goes to its composer. Hosts on 0.4.3 also let the iPhone app show the images a message carried.

## What's new

### Desktop

- **Your phone stays quiet while you're here.** While you're active at the Mac (no lock, no screensaver, input in the last 3 minutes), each host you're connected to holds pushes to your linked phones. Anything you handle at the Mac never pushes; anything still waiting goes to your phone once you step away. Turn it off in Settings → Sessions ("Quiet my phone while I'm at this computer").
- **⌘N focuses the prompt.** A new session's prompt is ready to type in, and ⌘N on a new-session page already open focuses it again.
- **Type to reply.** On a session screen, start typing anywhere outside a text field or terminal and it goes to the composer. Single-key shortcuts still win: `k` opens Jump to…, while `Shift+K` types a capital K.

### Linked devices

- **Images on the phone.** A host on 0.4.3 lets a linked phone fetch the images a message carried, so they show in the phone's thread wherever they were sent from. Only images can be fetched this way, by their attachment, never by a path on disk; Extensions can't use it.

### The iPhone app (TestFlight)

These are in build 6.

- A constellation opens as its lead's thread, with its tasks in a panel at the top that opens and closes; you can message the lead whatever state the constellation is in.
- A message you send while a turn runs shows at once ("Steering…"), then as "Steered this turn" where the agent took it in, or says why it didn't land.
- A message's images show above its bubble, queued or sent; a queued message shows in the thread until it goes.
- A brief reconnect, such as coming back to the app, no longer flashes "Connecting…" or dims the screen.
- Home's horizon glows softly instead of showing a solid shape.

## Installing

**Mac (Apple silicon).** Download `Polaris-0.4.3-arm64.dmg` and drag Polaris to Applications, then open it.

- **On 0.4.2 or earlier,** Polaris updates itself. Choose **Restart to update** when it says 0.4.3 is ready.

**Windows.** The Windows build still isn't signed, so SmartScreen will warn you the first time. Download `Polaris-0.4.3-x64-win-Setup.exe` (or the `arm64` one for ARM PCs) and run it. When SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

- **On 0.2.0 or later,** Polaris updates itself.

**Your hosts.** With **Keep daemons up to date** on (Settings → Hosts), each host moves to 0.4.3 the next time it connects. Holding phone pushes, and images sent from another device showing on the phone, need the host on 0.4.3.

## Known limitations

- Pushes a host is holding are lost if it restarts before you step away.
- The iPhone app is in TestFlight, by invitation.
- 0.3.0's [known limitations](https://github.com/luxdotdev/polaris/releases/tag/v0.3.0) still apply.
