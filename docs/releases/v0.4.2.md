A better phone: the iPhone app's threads stay live over the Polaris Relay, and hosts on 0.4.2 let a linked phone pin, rename and archive agent sessions and pause, resume, complete or archive a constellation. On the Mac, the relay route stops saying "Connecting…" once it's connected.

## What's new

### Linked devices

- **More from your phone.** A linked phone may now pin, rename, archive and unarchive agent sessions, and pause, resume, complete or archive a constellation. Archiving from a phone never deletes the session's branch, so Unarchive always undoes it, and a phone can't hand a constellation's lead to another harness.

### Desktop

- **The Polaris Relay route shows Connected.** Settings → Hosts no longer says "Connecting…" after the host has reached the relay; it updates on its own.

### The iPhone app (TestFlight)

The app updates through TestFlight on its own schedule; these land in its next build.

- Open threads stay live: a relay connection that dies without closing is found within seconds, and the thread catches up.
- Tool calls fold into runs, as on the Mac ("Read 3 files, edited 1 file, ran 2 commands"), and a call in flight shows what it's doing.
- Agent messages render as Markdown.
- Paste an image into a composer to attach it.
- Long-press a session, constellation or host for its actions.
- The Working dither matches the Mac's, animated.
- A constellation's lead opens its own thread, where you can keep talking to it after the constellation is finished.
- Swipe back from the left edge; the constellation view clears the Dynamic Island.

## Installing

**Mac (Apple silicon).** Download `Polaris-0.4.2-arm64.dmg` and drag Polaris to Applications, then open it.

- **On 0.4.1 or earlier,** Polaris updates itself. Choose **Restart to update** when it says 0.4.2 is ready.

**Windows.** The Windows build still isn't signed, so SmartScreen will warn you the first time. Download `Polaris-0.4.2-x64-win-Setup.exe` (or the `arm64` one for ARM PCs) and run it. When SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

- **On 0.2.0 or later,** Polaris updates itself.

**Your hosts.** With **Keep daemons up to date** on (Settings → Hosts), each host moves to 0.4.2 the next time it connects. The phone's new long-press actions need its hosts on 0.4.2.
