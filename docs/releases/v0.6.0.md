The iPhone app gets the night sky. Home, threads, Review, constellations, Settings and pairing are redrawn on the same Moonlit and Midnight themes as the Mac, with a celestial for every agent session. Agent sessions now follow you onto the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island with a Live Activity. On the Mac, subagents open in their own tab, the conversation follows the latest turn.

## What's new

### The iPhone app (TestFlight)

These are in build 8, version 0.6.0.

- **Moonlit and Midnight.** Settings → Appearance picks the theme, as on the Mac, and Motion can turn the animation down. Every screen sits on the night sky, with the horizon along its foot.
- **Home.** Agent sessions are rows with their celestials, and whatever needs you leads at the top, swiping between requests. The badge opens the Needs You inbox from any screen.
- **Threads.** The composer has one control row and a model sheet for harness, model, effort and permissions. Finished turns show their receipts, and a long press on a turn opens its menu.
- **A new session starts in the composer.** New opens a blank thread with your last workspace and model; the first send starts the session and its celestial rises into the header. A host with no harness ready says how to set one up.
- **Review.** The queue puts Critical findings first. A turn's review pages through findings by severity, asks the reviewer or walks through the change, and opens full-screen diffs you can swipe between, split side by side in landscape.
- **Constellations.** A constellation's header is a star chart of its tasks, with a task sheet, the claim and stats sheets, and the completion card.
- **Pairing.** Link this iPhone and Link another Mac share one scan sheet, with the link pasted instead when the camera can't see the code. Hosts live under Settings, each with a details sheet and its own Unlink.
- **When a host is away.** Home and Review say they can't reach your hosts and show the host's own reason. A message you send meanwhile waits as queued and goes when the host is back; one that may not have arrived is marked, with Send again.
- **Live Activity and Dynamic Island.** While agent sessions work, the Lock Screen and the Island show how many and for how long, with the newest step. When one needs you, it takes over with the request, and you can approve or deny it from there.
- **Widgets** follow the app's theme. Notifications lead with the host's name, so it survives truncation.
- **Idle compaction** shows as a quiet line in the thread, not as your own /compact.
- **Sturdier.** Every new screen was tested with long names, many hosts and the largest text sizes.

### Desktop

- **Subagents** open in Output's Subagent tab, and the moons and Waiting on share one roster.
- **The conversation follows the latest turn,** with an arrow back to it once you scroll up. Claude Code's own tools read in words, and web sources gather into one pill.
- **The agent's todo list** shares the notice strip with Working, and opens to its checklist.
- **/ and @ in the composer.** / opens Skills wherever a word starts, and @ mentions a file in the workspace.
- **Commands in full.** Hovering a command shows all of it.
- **Usage.** Pin a day to narrow the by-model table to it.
- **Workspaces.** A hidden workspace can be removed from Settings, which removes its agent sessions and constellations too.
- **The window** reopens at its last size and position, maximized or full screen.
- **The completion card** is the Stats card at rest.

### Your hosts

- **Idle compaction.** An agent session with at least 200k tokens of context, idle for 55 minutes, is compacted while its prompt cache is still warm, so the next turn starts cheaper. Settings → Sessions turns it off; it's on by default.
- **Constellation workers stay quiet.** They leave the session lists on the Mac and the iPhone, and push only when they need you.
- **Live Activity updates** come from each host, sealed so the relay never sees what they say.

## Installing

**Mac (Apple silicon).** Download `Polaris-0.6.0-arm64.dmg` and drag Polaris to Applications, then open it.

- **On 0.5.0 or earlier,** Polaris updates itself. Choose **Restart to update** when it says 0.6.0 is ready.

**Windows.** The Windows build still isn't signed, so SmartScreen will warn you the first time. Download `Polaris-0.6.0-x64-win-Setup.exe` (or the `arm64` one for ARM PCs) and run it. When SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

- **On 0.2.0 or later,** Polaris updates itself.

**Your hosts.** With **Keep daemons up to date** on (Settings → Hosts), each host moves to 0.6.0 the next time it connects. The Live Activity, idle compaction and quiet constellation workers need the host on 0.6.0.

## Known limitations

- While the iPhone app is in the background, the Live Activity is updated by pushes from your hosts; until those are proven against Apple's servers, it may only catch up when you open the app.
- On the iPhone, the Hosts list squeezes long names at the largest text sizes, and a long command can be cut short in the Live Activity's takeover.
- The iPhone app is in TestFlight, by invitation.
- 0.3.0's [known limitations](https://github.com/luxdotdev/polaris/releases/tag/v0.3.0) still apply.
