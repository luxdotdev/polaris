## Decisions worth repeating

### The mark is an even pixel star
- What: symmetric 16×16 Nether-star-style mark (option B: slim body, short
  diagonals), shaded at 24px and up, flat below; generated by
  `design/scripts/gen_dither.py`.
- Evidence: owner: "Our star is a bit uneven... Minecraft Nether star";
  "A is a bit too chunky. B is good though, C is a bit too narrow."
- Repeat when: drawing the mark anywhere; never redraw by hand, rotate, or
  anti-alias it.

### Wordmark in Geist Pixel, everything else in SF Pro
- What: Geist Pixel Square for the wordmark only; headlines and body SF Pro
  medium/regular; code SF Mono.
- Evidence: owner: "lean into Geist Pixel"; DESIGN.md, Brand.
- Repeat when: any brand or marketing surface.

### The north star idea, stated plainly
- What: "The north star for your agents."; principles calm, precise,
  alive; agents as stars steering by one fixed point.
- Evidence: owner: "the shining beacon that leads your agents to success";
  deck slides 01–03. The Constellation name follows the same metaphor.
- Repeat when: naming features and writing marketing copy. Prefer the sky
  metaphor where it is literal (constellation = graph of tasks), never as
  decoration.
